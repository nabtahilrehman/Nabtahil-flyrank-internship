FlyRank Content Problem Connection
Case Study Context

This project focuses on the FlyRank content challenge of identifying pages that may represent SEO opportunities from a large collection of indexed content.

As websites grow, manually reviewing every page becomes difficult and resource intensive. The objective of this project was to investigate whether historical search-performance signals could support content prioritization.

Using observed metrics such as impressions, clicks, and average position, the project developed a decision-support framework that helps identify pages that may warrant further SEO review.

The findings should be interpreted as observed and directional evidence rather than guarantees of future performance improvements.
